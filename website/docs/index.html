New to Neuron Flame?

Start with Quick Start, then read Permissions. Most people are productive within a minute. Once you're comfortable with push-to-talk, explore Power Mode — it's the feature you didn't know you needed.

Coming from another dictation app?

If you're switching from Apple's built-in dictation or another voice-to-text tool, Neuron Flame's hotkey-driven flow will feel familiar but faster. The key differences:

  • Push-to-talk by default — hold the key while speaking, release when done. No "wake word", no timeout.
  • Local-first — Parakeet V2 runs on your Apple Neural Engine in milliseconds. No cloud latency.
  • Cursor-aware paste — text appears wherever your blinking caret already was.
  • AI enhancement is optional and separate — the raw transcript is always saved, even if enhancement fails or is off.
