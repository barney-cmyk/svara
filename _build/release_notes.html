v1.89 — Filter the Local catalogue, see when models have a newer version

  • Filter chips at the top of the Local tab. Three groups, multi-select, hide what you don't want to see:
    • Trust — Verified / Community / Custom
    • Organisation — OpenAI / NVIDIA / Apple / HuggingFace / etc. — adapts to whoever's models are actually available
    • Family — Whisper / Parakeet / Apple Speech
    Click "Filter" to expand, click chips to toggle. "Clear" wipes all selections.
  • "Newer version available" banner appears below any downloaded model that's been superseded by a newer one (e.g. you have Parakeet V2 but V3 exists). One click downloads the newer version and sets it as default — no hunting through the list.

The underlying data comes from the remote catalogue (neuronflame.com/models.json), so when NVIDIA ships Parakeet V4, anyone on V3 sees the upgrade banner within 24 hours of me adding the entry.
