v1.80 — In-app update notifications

You no longer have to remember to check for updates manually. Neuron Flame now does it for you, quietly:

  • Background check on launch + hourly while open. Sparkle pings neuronflame.com/appcast.xml in the background. No popup, no interruption.
  • "Update available" pill in the sidebar. When a new version is found, a small Liquid-Glass pill appears under the Neuron Flame wordmark — soft brand-purple background, gentle pulsing dot, version number. Click it to install.
  • No nag. When you're up to date, the sidebar stays exactly the same. No permanent version badge, no notification dot, nothing.

From v1.81 onwards (next release), you'll see the new behaviour in action — the pill will appear under the brand mark within a few seconds of opening the app.
