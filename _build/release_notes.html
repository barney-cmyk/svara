v1.87 — Smarter "Recommended" tab

The Recommended tab is no longer a hand-picked list of four models. It's now computed live from whatever's in the merged registry (built-in + remote catalogue + your custom endpoints), and each pick carries a small badge explaining why it's there:

  • BEST OVERALL — top score on the formula accuracy² × speed
  • FASTEST — highest speed among local (on-device) models
  • MOST ACCURATE — highest accuracy regardless of speed
  • BEST FOR MULTILINGUAL — top scorer among multilingual models
  • BEST CLOUD — top scorer among cloud-hosted options

Practical effect: when a new model lands in models.json with better metrics than any current pick, the Recommended tab updates automatically — no app release needed. Users always see the best models for their use case, not the ones that were "best" six months ago.
